R2-D2 Wiring Reference: Simplified Droid

Wire with all power disconnected. Check the physical pin markings on every connector before fitting it.

The receiver drives both VESC halves directly. The dome ESP32 reads the receiver's iBUS and runs the dome servo, sound and holo servos. The body and dome 5V positives never connect; grounds are common through slip ring CH2.
