R2-D2 Power Harness and Pin-to-Pin Inspector

Schematic layout. Always verify physical connector pin markings before wiring.

Level shifting & Serial: The FlySky receiver is mounted in the dome. Receiver iBUS and dome Hall signals step down from 5V to 3.3V before entering the ESP32. VESC Drive UART (GPIO18 / AUX 4), dome PWM, and audio TX originate from the ESP32 at 3.3V and bypass the shifter.

Red: battery positive. Purple: body 5V. Pink: dome 5V. Dashed grey: ground return. I2C SDA/SCL: blue/green. Motor phases U/V/W: orange/violet/blue. Hall H1/H2/H3: yellow/blue/green. Holo CH0-5: yellow/blue/green/violet/orange/pink. Cyan: slip-ring paths. Colors identify diagram circuits, not required physical wire jackets.

Do not join the two 5V positive rails. Do not fuse ground. Leave ring CH3 (spare), VESC COMM 5V/3.3V/TX, and motor temperature leads unconnected.
