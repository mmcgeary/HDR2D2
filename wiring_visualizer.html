CH4/5 are insulated spares.

USB / power: VUSB/VIN trace isolation is approved but not physically done. Follow the Teensy 4.1 underside cut and unpowered multimeter checks in the body wiring guide; verify no carrier re-bridge. After isolation, external VIN is required even with normal USB attached. Keep independent 5V positives and common grounds.

Assembly / shopping / acceptance guide

Harness inspector

Click any wire or component to inspect its terminals, gauge, fuse protection, and assembly notes. Selected wires are highlighted in the diagram. Drag the canvas to pan; use the zoom buttons or mouse wheel to zoom.

Diagram colors: Red: positive supplies (12V, 5V, 3.3V). Dashed black with a light outline: ground. Body and dome 5V positives are still independent; color does not imply continuity.

Body-link CH3: cyan; CH6: amber. Left VESC TX/RX: sky blue/indigo. Right VESC TX/RX: lime/green. DFPlayer command/feedback: pink/violet. Receiver iBUS: yellow; SENSOR: peach. Dome-servo pulses: lavender; home Hall: mint. Each signal keeps its color across ring contacts and both sides of its translator.

I2C SDA/SCL: blue/teal. Motor phases U/V/W: orange/violet/blue. Motor Hall H1/H2/H3: yellow/blue/green. Holo CH0-5: yellow/blue/green/violet/orange/pink. Hover/focus thickens a wire; selected wires glow. Signal colors are diagram tracing aids, not physical wire-jacket requirements.

Four owned inline 5V holders: Body servo 5A; body electronics 2A; dome PCA9685 V+ 5A; dome electronics/lighting 3A. No 5V fuse boxes. Grounds unfused. VESC COMM power/ADC, single RECEIVER connector, CAN/SWD and F2/F6 unused. BTL speaker: neither lead is ground. Analog isolator remains intact.
